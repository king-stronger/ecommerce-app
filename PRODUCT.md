# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Portfolio visitors and future clients reviewing a demo shop. They should feel they could hire the builder for a real ecommerce platform.

## Product Purpose

A storefront that shows the full public surface of the ecommerce API: catalog, guest cart, account, shipping, and coupon. The home and product pages must look like a real fashion shop, not a wireframe.

## Positioning

FORMA is a thin client of the existing OpenAPI backend. Guest cart and checkout already exist on the API. The storefront does not invent payments, reviews, search, wishlist, or membership.

## Operating Context

Local demo. API on port 8787, SvelteKit on 4173. Admin stays in Scalar. UI language is English. Currency is EUR (backend cents).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live: public catalog, guest cart cookie, checkout routes, order lookup, Better Auth account, shipping methods, coupon WELCOME10.
- Not live: search, wishlist, reviews, lookbook, financing, newsletter membership.
- Brand name: FORMA.
- Visual direction is a structural copy of the approved fashion homepage mock (promo bar, centered wordmark, lifestyle hero, circular category row, asymmetric edit, mid banner, bestseller grid). Product detail follows the approved PDP anatomy (gallery, options, quantity, dual CTAs, shipping facts) without inventing backend features.

## Brand Commitments

Quiet fashion shop: cream ground, sand surfaces, near-black type and primary actions, rounded media. Serif display for the wordmark and hero; sans for UI. Real Unsplash product photography only. Structure over decoration.

## Evidence on Hand

Seeded English categories and products with photos, Standard (€4.99) and Express (€9.99) shipping, WELCOME10 (10% off from €20, cap €15). Do not invent reviews, free-shipping thresholds the API does not enforce, or brand claims beyond seeded copy.

## Product Principles

- One clear next step: browse → product → cart.
- Real catalog data only; density high enough that the shop feels stocked.
- Header: promo, categories, FORMA, account, cart.
- Admin and payment stay out of this storefront.
